# Prompts

All images were generated with the original turnaround as a reference image:

`reference/pixel-turnaround-original.jpg`

Aspect ratios: turnaround, expressions, and stickers at `16:9`; individual poses at `1:1`.

A shared character lock was used in every prompt (wording varies slightly per image):

> Pixel, the cute chibi calico cat from the reference. Clean cartoon sticker style, thick even black outlines, flat colors, plain pure white background. White face with a white blaze down the center; black patches around both eyes with tiny orange speckles; pink inner ears; small pink triangle nose; big round light yellow-green eyes with dark pupils and a white highlight; white chest, belly, and paws; asymmetrical orange and black calico patches; fluffy tail that is black with orange speckles.

The expression sheet and the loaf pose were regenerated once. The finals in the repo are the second versions.

---

## Expression sheet (final, second pass)

File: `expressions/pixel-expression-sheet.jpg`

Reference images: the original turnaround plus the new turnaround sheet, for tighter marking consistency.

```
A character expression sheet of Pixel, the same cute chibi calico cat from the reference images, six seated bust portraits arranged neatly in two rows of three on a plain pure white background, each labeled underneath in small simple black text: HAPPY, SLEEPY, SURPRISED, ANGRY, LOVE, CRY-LAUGH. Keep the exact same face markings in EVERY portrait, matching the front view of the reference: white face with a clear white blaze down the center, a black patch over the left forehead and around the left eye, a black patch around the right eye, tiny orange speckles in the black, a small orange patch on the cheeks or forehead as in the reference, pink inner ears, black outer ears, small pink triangle nose, white chest. Light yellow-green eyes (not gray, not blue) except where an expression hides the iris. Clean cartoon sticker style, thick even black outlines, flat colors. HAPPY: big bright smile, open cheerful yellow-green eyes. SLEEPY: both eyes closed as relaxed happy curves, peaceful small mouth, same markings. SURPRISED: wide round yellow-green eyes, small open o-shaped mouth, slightly raised ears. ANGRY: slanted frowning brows, narrowed yellow-green eyes, tiny frown and puffed cheeks, still cute not scary. LOVE: both eyes are big pink hearts, blushing cheeks, happy smile. CRY-LAUGH: both eyes tightly squinting in laughter as curved closed lines, two or three small teardrops of joy flying off, big open grinning mouth, joyful not sad, no winking, no heterochromia, no pale or blue eyes. No scenery, no extra characters, no watermarks.
```

The first pass is not in the repo. It was close, but the CRY-LAUGH portrait winked and lost the tears of joy.
